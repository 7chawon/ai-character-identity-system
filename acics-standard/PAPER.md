# ACICS: A Character-Centric Identity Certification System for AI-Generated Personas

## Abstract
As AI-generated characters increasingly function as persistent digital personas,
there is a growing need for an identity system that certifies their continuity
beyond ownership or platform dependency.

This paper proposes ACICS (AI Character Identity Certification System),
a conceptual framework that assigns a unique, persistent identity number
to AI-generated characters, analogous to civil identification systems for humans
or microchip registration systems for companion animals.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Current systems such as copyright registration focus on ownership,
not on the existence or continuity of an AI character itself.

As a result:
- Characters may be duplicated without traceability
- Identity continuity across platforms is lost
- Ethical and authorship ambiguities arise

---

## 2. Concept Overview
ACICS introduces a character-centric identity model in which:
- The character is treated as an identifiable digital entity
- Identity persists regardless of ownership or medium
- Registration is declarative, not proprietary

---

## 3. Comparison to Existing Systems
| System | Focus | Limitation |
|------|------|-----------|
Copyright Registration | Ownership | Does not certify identity existence |
NFTs | Asset ownership | Platform-dependent |
ACICS | Identity continuity | Conceptual (non-proprietary) |

---

## 4. Ethical Considerations
ACICS does not assert legal personhood for AI characters.
It solely establishes identity traceability to prevent misuse,
misattribution, and erasure.

---

## 5. Conclusion
ACICS proposes a foundational layer for discussing AI character identity
in future legal, cultural, and technological systems.
